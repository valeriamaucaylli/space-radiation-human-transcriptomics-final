# Human omics governance and data availability

This archive provides analysis code and **four-pathway-level** aggregate result tables only. It does not include astronaut-level count matrices, donor/sample-level scores, metadata linking individual observations, raw human reads or processed subject-identifying derivatives. Public availability at a source repository does not automatically grant redistribution rights. NASA/OSDR/GeneLab/GEO terms and any human research governance restrictions must be verified for each underlying dataset.

The newly supplied `cellular_counts_raw (1).parquet`, `cellular_sample_metadata (1).parquet`, `cfRNA_jaxa.parquet` and duplicates are intentionally **excluded**. Parquet content could not be inspected here because a compatible Parquet reader was unavailable; publication and privacy clearance remain unverified. The `cfRNA_jaxa` duplicate filenames have identical SHA-256 hashes, but this does not validate their contents. Other source OSDR/GEO raw/normalized expression matrices and ISA ZIPs are also **not** mirrored in these publication candidates.

The authors must review whether even aggregate tables/figures are approved for distribution and obtain coauthor consent before public release. File-level SHA-256 hashes of locally held sources are in the private-only audit record.
