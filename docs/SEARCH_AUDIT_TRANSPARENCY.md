# 00A literature search / novelty audit — documentation status

Historical project conversation checkpoints state that PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus and Web of Science exports were screened and that the novelty review was concluded on 17 October 2026. The project-record summary reports 5,693 retrieved references, 3,817 after DOI/title deduplication and 509 sensitively screened records. These numbers derive from conversational reporting; **their original exports and screening logs were not present among files supplied for this release, and have not been independently verified here**.

**No publication-date restriction was imposed by the investigators in any bibliographic database.** The institutional Web of Science access presented records from 2016 onward; this is a database coverage limitation, **not** a publication-year eligibility filter. LeapSpace was not searched because paid access was unavailable. Supplemental web/preprint checks do not count as a LeapSpace search. A future final supplementary novelty-search audit should attach the original queries and dated exports where sharing is permitted.

No discovery claim of a radiation-specific transcriptomic signature is justified. The defensible contribution is pre-specified cross-context pathway triangulation with explicit limits on radiological attribution.
